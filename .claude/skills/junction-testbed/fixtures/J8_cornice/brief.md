# J8: cornice around a tower

- `Tower`: closed box, x -2 to 2, y -2 to 2, z 0 to 8.
- Cornice: a band projecting 0.3 m from every tower face, z 6.0 to 6.25, running all the way around, corners included.
  Build it as one closed part named `Cornice` or as several closed parts named `Cornice.<something>`.

The cornice sits against the tower faces along its whole back (gap at most 5 mm, at most 2 cm into the tower). At the
corners the band is continuous: every corner is filled, and cornice parts never overlap each other (at most 5 mm).
