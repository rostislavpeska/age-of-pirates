# J2: crossing hip roofs

Two hip roofs crossing each other (roofs only, no walls). Both: pitch 35 degrees, eaves at z = 3 above the wall line,
0.6 m overhang beyond the wall line.

- `Roof.Main`: sheet. Wall line x -5 to 5, y -3 to 3, ridge along x.
- `Roof.Cross`: sheet. Wall line x -2 to 2, y -6 to 6, ridge along y.
