# J5: beam from an angled wall to a post

- `Wall`: closed box 0.4 m thick, 6 m long, z 0 to 3.2. Its east face is the vertical plane through the origin with
  outward normal (cos 20 deg, -sin 20 deg, 0); the wall lies behind that face and runs 3 m each way from the origin
  along it.
- `Post`: closed box 0.25 x 0.25, centred at x = 4, y = 0, z 0 to 2.8.
- `Beam`: closed, 0.2 m wide (y -0.1 to 0.1), 0.25 m high (z 2.8 to 3.05), from the wall to x = 4.325, resting on
  the post.
