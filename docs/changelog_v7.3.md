# Version 7.3: Restoration of the Monarchy & Revelations of Istanbul

Autumn 2026 release. Two new historical city maps lead it: London 1660, with two new native factions (the Parliamentarians and an extended House of Stuart), and Istanbul 1512, held back from 7.2 together with its Ottoman content. A third new map, the Danube Bend, joins them, along with the cliff-unloading option from the October game update on twelve maps and a long list of fixes.

## Maps
- **Restoration of the Monarchy (London 1660)** — Royalist attackers (House of Stuart) against Parliament's defenders, with the Thames between the two banks of the city. Team Victory: capture both Royal Keeps, the twin castles on the river, and hold them together for eight minutes. Each Keep is guarded by Redcoats or Whitecoats and opens once its guard is beaten. Whoever holds the Toll Station on London Bridge holds the crossing, its gates and its battery towers.
  - St Paul's Cathedral and the Minster each research one of London's two trade-route upgrades: St Pancras Station turns the land route into Trains, the East India Trading Company turns the Thames route into East Indiamen. The first player to research one upgrades that route for everyone. Team London Ferry System and Blessed City are researched at the cathedrals.
  - Two pirate havens at the mouth of the Thames, one on each bank. Bass and salmon in the Thames, which is its own river water.
  - Local mercenaries of the Civil Wars: Harquebusier, Armored Pistoleer, Highlander, Gallowglass, Li'l Bombard; outlaws: Highwayman and Sailor. The city is held with barricades, not stone walls (no Bastion, Redoubts or City Fortifications). No Trade Monopoly victory.
  - Recommended civs (the Three Kingdoms War set): British, Danes, Dutch, French, Portuguese, Spanish. Settlements: House of Stuart, Parliament, Jewish Quarter, Pirates.
- **Revelations of Istanbul (Istanbul 1512)** — Selim against Ahmed for their father's throne: two teams, two shores, the Bosporus between them. Team Victory: capture the two Naval Forts (Black Sea and Mediterranean) and the two Prince's Palaces, all guarded, and hold all four together.
  - Built for team play, ideally 2 vs 2: trade harbours, factories, pirate camps and Fisherman's Guilds are capturable and shared with your team. Ferry Harbours carry garrisoned units across the strait; Team Istanbul Ferry System makes their deploy recharge 30% faster for the whole team. Every player starts with a Dock Builder.
  - Local mercenaries: Bosniak, Stradiot, Mameluke, Zenata Rider, Li'l Bombard; outlaws: Hajduk and Corsair Marksman.
  - Settlements: Sufi, Orthodox, House of Phanar, House of Auditore, Pirates, Cossacks.
- **Danube** — land map on the Danube Bend. The river closes the land inside the bend on three sides: only the bridges lead to the castles of the Prince Electors and their rich mines. Every player starts at a fort. Settlements: Prince Electors, Hussites, Orthodox, Jesuits.

### Map updates
- **Cliff unloading** — the vanilla cliff-unloading option from the October 2026 game update is switched on for Paris, London, Iceland, Istanbul, Venice City, Elbe, Caribbean Wars, Black Sea, Malta, Malta Castles, The First Cold War and Tasmania.
- **City maps** — houses are limited per civ instead of forbidden: Danes, Africans, Haudenosaunee, Inca and Swedish Torps 5 each, Japanese shrines 5, Chinese villages 3 (British manors and the other European houses stay forbidden). Japanese shrines gather 100% faster, Torps 50% faster. Danish houses and churches, the Customs House, the Polish church, the Sejm and the Folwark follow the city placement rules. Military Camps train Polish (Piechur, Winged Hussar, Lithuanian Rider) and Danish (Snaphaner) units.
- **Black Sea** — the terrain pass held back from 7.2: a hillier countryside with cliff-top patches.
- **Independence War** — local mercenaries (Hessian Jaeger, Highlander, Irish Brigadier, Fusilier) and American outlaws (Owlhoot, Desperado). The Distillery is now the Whiskey Distillery.
- **Storming the Bastille and March to Versailles** — Poles recommended (Kościuszko's uprising, the Polish Legions of the French Republic). The Versailles cathedral block now holds St Paul's as its Metropolitan Cathedral.
- **King of Bohemia** — Danes recommended (Christian IV's intervention); Russians no longer recommended.
- **Caribbean Wars** — pirate city states reworked: new harbour props and repaved beach streets.
- **Unknown map** — the House of Stuart joins the Age of Pirates exclusive natives; the extended Inuits, House of Phanar and House of Stuart now work on this map.
- **Labrador Coast** — Great Lakes ice water.

## Natives
### Parliamentarians (new, London defenders)
- **Lord** — Parliamentarian nobleman on horseback (650 HP, 300F/300G, build limit 2). He trains Parliamentarian units in the field, builds Trading Posts and barricades, collects treasures, and rises from Lord Commander to Lord Lieutenant to Lord General. Good against artillery and heavy infantry.
- **Redcoat** — New Model Army foot, musket and bayonet, good against cavalry (160 HP, 75F/25G, build limit 13). Trained at the Trading Post.
- Improvements: Excise Ordinance (a small coin trickle), Trained Bands (settlers +40% hitpoints and attack), Lines of Communication (barricades +50% hitpoints; Redcoats and other Parliamentarian units can build them), Eastern Association (a Lord command aura of +15% attack and +10% hitpoints; several Lords do not stack), Veteran Parliamentarians and Parliamentarian Guard.
- **The Grand Remonstrance** — pick your leader of Parliament (defenders only); each brings his own regiment and two techs, and raises the Commonwealth's flag over your city:
  - **Oliver Cromwell** — Ironside cavalry (260 HP, 100F/90G). Ironside Levy ships 12 Ironsides. New Model Army: Redcoats +30% hitpoints and attack, 25% faster training, +10 build limit; Musketeers +10%.
  - **Lord Inchiquin** — Bonaght Soldier (155 HP, 70F/25W). Munster Levies ships an Armed Merchantman with 12 Bonaghts. Team Adventurers' Act: 1 coin per second for every team member.
  - **Sir Thomas Myddelton** — Welsh Longbowman (95 HP, 60F/40W). Welsh Drovers ships 8 Sheep, and livestock fatten 25% faster. Team New River Company: captured city resource buildings work 50% faster for the whole team.
- All Parliamentarian units are also available at the Native Embassy.

### House of Stuart (extended)
- **Divine Right of Kings** — new big button (attackers only on London):
  - **Sovereign of the Seas** ships the King's flagship, and every Dock builds Regal Ships from then on (up to 2).
  - **The Last Cavalier** sends Prince Rupert of the Rhine with 10 Cavaliers. Rupert is a Royalist cavalry hero (1200 HP) who builds Trading Posts, collects treasures, trains Cavaliers up to 10 and returns after being defeated; Rupert's Horse makes cavalry within 24 metres 15% faster.
- Highland Charge moved to a compact ability button.
- London map bonus: every Lowlander becomes a **Whitecoat** — the Marquess of Newcastle's Royalist foot (170 HP, 55F/45G), tougher than the Lowlander and with pike-length reach. Whitecoats build barricades and Military Camps once the extension is unlocked; Veteran, Guard and Imperial Whitecoat.

### Inuits
- The **Harpooner** now replaces the Inuit Hunter at the Trading Post once the Inuits are extended, and every Hunter you already have takes up the harpoon. Build limit 12, lighter (150 HP, was 240) but hitting far harder than a hunting bow. He inherits the Hunter's upgrades and hunting promotion and gains a Stand Ground stance. Hunters sent from the Home City still arrive as Hunters.

### Cossacks
- Slavonic military voices, an attack acknowledgement for the rowboat warship, new Haidamaka dragoon rider art.

### Pirates
- Captain Beauregard is back in the Baltic pirate captain choice (Iceland, Elbe, London offered only two captains).
- Pirate villages updated: a new Nassau cabin set, a better-scaled Tortuga cabin, an updated Tortuga pub, and five villages rebuilt.

## New units and content
- **Treasure Ship** — new model with furling sails on the intact and the damaged ship, destruction, and player-colour sails (two stripes and a Chinese coin); 4500 HP, line of sight 40, its own ship names. The Chinese Imperial Navy card becomes **Captain Huang's Treasure Ship**: it ships a Treasure Ship and a Fire Junk.
- **Regal Ship** — new textures, its own icon and portrait, 2350 HP, build limit 2, and a voice for every civ.
- **Armed Merchantman** — the merchant sister of John Paul Jones' flagship under plain sails, shipped by Munster Levies.
- **St Paul's Cathedral and the Minster** — London's two cathedrals, with the cathedral techs (5000 HP, 200W/200G).
- **Royal Keep** — the Home City Tower of London as a capturable castle on the map (6500 HP).
- **London's skyline** — British Castle, British and Scottish palaces, Parliament church and tower, Stuart tower.
- **Timber Mill** — replaces the King's Forester, on the half-timbered lumber mill model.
- **Treasures** — the Gallows (an unused gallows worth resources) and the Royal Huntsman rock rescue in London's parks.
- **Main menu** — the Age of Pirates logo replaces the AoE3 logo.

## Other improvements
- Cathedrals are visible under fog and see further (line of sight 30): Metropolitan Cathedral, St Paul's, the Minster, Great Basilica, San Marco, Notre Dame, Sultan's Mosque, Royal Chapel, Hanseatic Church.
- 55 unit icons and portraits with a dark or dim player-colour area repaired.
- Every civ has ship voices and names on the Regal Ship and the Armed Merchantman (131 civs had none, including all revolutions and royal houses).
- Official parchment loading maps for Labrador Coast, Malta, Malta Castles, Black Sea, Iceland and The First Cold War; new parchment loading maps for Independence War and Istanbul.
- Trade routes on London and Istanbul start at level 1. Trading Posts do not upgrade them; London upgrades only through its cathedrals, and Istanbul's sea routes stay at level 1.
- Sufi and Jewish settlers no longer gather from pigs.
- Tooling (not player-facing): trigger data updated to the October game update, AI test harness, export and map checks.

## AI changes
- **London**: the AI plays the map — it builds behind its own city walls, attacks across the bridge, breaks blocking gates, holds and retakes the Royal Keeps, and sets its forward base at the enemy bridgehead once the crossing is open.
- **Storming the Bastille**: forward base at the enemy's construction block.
- **Istanbul**: the fleet split is back — pirate ships and River Monitors attack the Naval Guns while the other warships guard the Naval Forts. The first dock is built by the starting Dock Builder. The forward base goes to the enemy beach next to the Fisherman's Guild.
- AI pirate rules start wherever a pirate settlement or pirate city state stands (London's and Istanbul's pirate camps were missed).

## Bugfixes
- Ships captured trading posts, harbours and forts: since the base game's September patch most ships capture like herders, so any auto-converting building in range flipped to a passing ship. Ships capture nothing on mod maps any more, except where naval capture belongs (Istanbul and the naval King of the Hill modes) — fixed.
- Istanbul: every AI stopped with a script error at match start — fixed.
- Captured neutral buildings (Town Mill, Aztec Granary, Aztec Shrine, Menagerie, Safari, Fisherman's Guild, both Pirate Docks) could be deleted by their new owner — fixed.
- Harbour guard treasures sometimes spawned in the water on Independence War and Black Sea, which left the harbour uncapturable — fixed.
- The Treasure Ship's Broadside did nothing — fixed (it needs the Wokou treasure fleet tech).
- Destroyed Asian Markets left no rubble (a base-game gap) — fixed.
- Factory wings on London, Storming the Bastille and March to Versailles, London's park palace and the Minster's monastery wings could be selected — fixed; the same for the decorative small houses on Caribbean Wars.
- Artillery loads onto the new DLC whaling ships and the Corvette without the slow load delay, as on every other ship.
- "The Emperess" ship name — fixed to "The Empress".

## Game balance
- Harpooner reworked (see Inuits).
- Regal Ship: 2350 HP (was 2100), build limit 2.
- Deploy buildings: the Advanced Commanderies card adds 55 capacity to every deploy building.
