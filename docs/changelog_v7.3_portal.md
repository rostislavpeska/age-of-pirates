# Version 7.3: Historical Maps vol.3

Autumn 2026 release. Two new historical city maps lead it: London 1660, with two new native factions (the Parliamentarians and an extended House of Stuart), and Istanbul 1512, held back from 7.2 together with its Ottoman content. A third new map, the Danube Bend, joins them, along with the cliff-unloading option from the October game update on twelve maps and a long list of fixes.

## Maps
- **Restoration of the Monarchy (London 1660)** — Royalist attackers (House of Stuart) against Parliament's defenders, with the Thames between the two banks of the city. Team Victory: capture both Royal Keeps, the twin castles on the river, and hold them together for eight minutes. Whoever holds the Toll Station on London Bridge holds the crossing, its gates and its battery towers.
- London: St Paul's Cathedral and the Minster each research one of London's two trade-route upgrades: St Pancras Station turns the land route into Trains, the East India Trading Company turns the Thames route into East Indiamen.
- London: Recommended civs: British, Danes, Dutch, French, Portuguese, Spanish. Settlements: House of Stuart, Parliament, Jewish Quarter, Pirates.
- **Revelations of Istanbul (Istanbul 1512)** — Selim against Ahmed for their father's throne: two teams, two shores, the Bosporus between them. Team Victory: capture the two Naval Forts (Black Sea and Mediterranean) and the two Prince's Palaces, all guarded, and hold all four together.
- Istanbul: Built for team play, ideally 2 vs 2. Ferry Harbours carry garrisoned units across the strait. Every player starts with a Dock Builder.
- Istanbul: Settlements: Sufi, Orthodox, House of Phanar, House of Auditore, Pirates, Cossacks.
- **Danube** — land map on the Danube Bend. The river closes the land inside the bend on three sides: only the bridges lead to the castles of the Prince Electors and their rich mines. Every player starts at a fort. Settlements: Prince Electors, Hussites, Orthodox, Jesuits.

### Map updates
- **Cliff unloading** — the vanilla cliff-unloading option from the October 2026 game update is switched on for Paris, London, Iceland, Istanbul, Venice City, Elbe, Caribbean Wars, Black Sea, Malta, Malta Castles, The First Cold War and Tasmania.
- **City maps** — houses are limited per civ instead of forbidden. Military Camps train Polish and Danish units.
- **Black Sea** — a hillier countryside with cliff-top patches.
- **Independence War** — local mercenaries and American outlaws.
- **Unknown map** — the House of Stuart joins the Age of Pirates exclusive natives; the extended Inuits, House of Phanar and House of Stuart now work on this map.

## Natives
### Parliamentarians (new, London defenders)
- **Lord** — Parliamentarian nobleman on horseback. He trains Parliamentarian units in the field, builds Trading Posts and barricades, and collects treasures.
- **Redcoat** — New Model Army foot, musket and bayonet, good against cavalry. Trained at the Trading Post.
- **The Grand Remonstrance** — pick your leader of Parliament (defenders only): **Oliver Cromwell** (Ironside cavalry), **Lord Inchiquin** (Bonaght Soldier) or **Sir Thomas Myddelton** (Welsh Longbowman).

### House of Stuart (extended)
- **Divine Right of Kings** — new big button (attackers only on London). **Sovereign of the Seas** ships the King's flagship. **The Last Cavalier** sends Prince Rupert of the Rhine with 10 Cavaliers.
- London map bonus: every Lowlander becomes a **Whitecoat** — the Marquess of Newcastle's Royalist foot.

### Inuits
- The **Harpooner** now replaces the Inuit Hunter at the Trading Post once the Inuits are extended, and every Hunter you already have takes up the harpoon.

### Pirates
- Captain Beauregard is back in the Baltic pirate captain choice.

## New units and content
- **Treasure Ship** — new model with furling sails and player-colour sails. The Chinese Imperial Navy card becomes **Captain Huang's Treasure Ship**.
- **Regal Ship** — new textures, its own icon and portrait.
- **Armed Merchantman** — shipped by Munster Levies.
- **St Paul's Cathedral and the Minster** — London's two cathedrals.
- **Royal Keep** — the Home City Tower of London as a capturable castle on the map.
- **Timber Mill** — replaces the King's Forester.

## Other improvements
- Cathedrals are visible under fog and see further (line of sight 30): Metropolitan Cathedral, St Paul's, the Minster, Great Basilica, San Marco, Notre Dame, Sultan's Mosque, Royal Chapel, Hanseatic Church.
- 55 unit icons and portraits with a dark or dim player-colour area repaired.
- Every civ has ship voices and names on the Regal Ship and the Armed Merchantman (131 civs had none, including all revolutions and royal houses).
- Official parchment loading maps for Labrador Coast, Malta, Malta Castles, Black Sea, Iceland and The First Cold War; new parchment loading maps for Independence War and Istanbul.
- Trade routes on London and Istanbul start at level 1. Trading Posts do not upgrade them; London upgrades only through its cathedrals, and Istanbul's sea routes stay at level 1.
- Sufi and Jewish settlers no longer gather from pigs.

## AI changes
- **London**: the AI plays the map — it builds behind its own city walls, attacks across the bridge, breaks blocking gates, holds and retakes the Royal Keeps, and sets its forward base at the enemy bridgehead once the crossing is open.
- **Storming the Bastille**: forward base at the enemy's construction block.
- **Istanbul**: the fleet split is back — pirate ships and River Monitors attack the Naval Guns while the other warships guard the Naval Forts.

## Bugfixes
- Cliff boarding and landing: since a base-game update, ships could not land units on cliff coasts or take them back on board on Venice City, Tasmania and Iceland — fixed with the cliff unloading from the October 2026 game update (see Map updates).
- Ships captured trading posts, harbours and forts: since the base game's September patch most ships capture like herders, so any auto-converting building in range flipped to a passing ship. Ships capture nothing on mod maps any more, except where naval capture belongs (Istanbul and the naval King of the Hill modes) — fixed.
- Captured neutral buildings (Town Mill, Aztec Granary, Aztec Shrine, Menagerie, Safari, Fisherman's Guild, both Pirate Docks) could be deleted by their new owner — fixed.
- Harbour guard treasures sometimes spawned in the water on Independence War and Black Sea, which left the harbour uncapturable — fixed.
- The Treasure Ship's Broadside did nothing — fixed (it needs the Wokou treasure fleet tech).
- Destroyed Asian Markets left no rubble (a base-game gap) — fixed.
- Factory wings on Storming the Bastille and March to Versailles could be selected — fixed; the same for the decorative small houses on Caribbean Wars.
- Artillery loads onto the new DLC whaling ships and the Corvette without the slow load delay, as on every other ship.
- "The Emperess" ship name — fixed to "The Empress".
- Consulate on mod maps: a partner you had already used could not be chosen again, and End Relations worked only once — fixed.
- Revolting to Romania or Chile turned a vanilla consulate card back on on mod maps (vanilla Russian Allies in the Hussite picker on the Danube, for example) — fixed, the revolution now swaps it for the mod's own card (not yet confirmed in game).
- With the Koreans add-on, a Korean player's consulate was empty on every mod map except Burma — fixed.
- Unknown map: the Maya stayed vanilla (no Maya Influence button at the Trading Post) in every game that rolled the Bourbon alliance — fixed, every player now gets the extended Maya.
- AI players could field raw proxy units instead of the real unit (seen with Prince Elector Mounted Infantry and Brunswickers) — proxies now also convert at the Native Embassy (in beta testing, not yet confirmed in game).

## Game balance
- Harpooner reworked (see Inuits).
- Regal Ship: 2350 HP (was 2100), build limit 2.
- Deploy buildings: the Advanced Commanderies card adds 55 capacity to every deploy building.
