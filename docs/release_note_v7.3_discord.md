**Age of Pirates 7.3: Historical Maps vol.3**

Three new maps, new natives, new ships and buildings, and many fixes.

**NEW MAPS**
• Restoration of the Monarchy (London 1660): Royalists against Parliament across the Thames. Capture both Royal Keeps and hold them for eight minutes.
• Revelations of Istanbul (Istanbul 1512): Selim's team against Ahmed's across the Bosporus. Capture both Naval Forts and both Prince's Palaces and hold all four.
• Danube: only the bridges lead to the castles of the Prince Electors and their rich mines.

**NEW CONTENT**
• Parliamentarians: London's defenders. Pick Oliver Cromwell, Lord Inchiquin or Sir Thomas Myddelton as your leader.
• House of Stuart, extended: the King's flagship Sovereign of the Seas and Prince Rupert of the Rhine.
• Harpooner: replaces the Inuit Hunter once the Inuits are extended.
• Ships and buildings: a new Treasure Ship with furling player-colour sails, a retextured Regal Ship, St Paul's Cathedral, the Minster and the Royal Keep.

**UPDATES AND FIXES**
• The AI now plays London: it attacks across the bridge, then holds and retakes the Royal Keeps.
• Cliff boarding and landing work again on Venice City, Tasmania and Iceland; cliff unloading is on for twelve maps.
• Unknown map update: the House of Stuart joins the exclusive natives.
• Passing ships no longer capture trading posts, harbours and forts, except on Istanbul and naval King of the Hill.
• Consulate partners can be chosen again, and End Relations works more than once.
