# Version 7.4

Draft, started 2026-11-11: the release after 7.3. Title and date are open.

## Bugfixes
- Volcano maps (Polynesia, Hawaii, Melanesia): the lava trails stayed invisible during eruptions, and the eruption did not damage units around the crater — fixed (confirmed in game on Polynesia).

## Game balance
- London: British players cannot build Manors on London, so the two big buttons now carry a British civ bonus, shown to British players as a green "Civ Bonus (British)" line (confirmed in game):
  - Divine Right of Kings (attackers): +10 maximum population.
  - The Grand Remonstrance (defenders): +50% build limit for Parliament's units (Redcoat 13 → 20, Lord 2 → 3, Ironside 10 → 15, Bonaght 15 → 22, Welsh Longbowman 15 → 22).
